I build desktop and web applications, experiment with AI, and enjoy solving problems in programming competitions. I've also gained industry experience through a summer internship at [MIC](https://www.mic-cust.com/).

## Achievements

- **1st overall out of 12 teams** at the MLH x YAIL Hacktoberfest HackDay in Linz, 2026. Our team built [Tripwire](https://github.com/LPuehringerStudent/Hacktoberfest-2026#results) and won **Best Use of Gemma 4** and **Best Open-Source AI Project**.
- **2nd in Linz and 11th worldwide** at the 42nd Cloudflight Coding Contest.

## Projects

### [QuizForge](https://github.com/MohamadAbdulwahid/QuizForge)

A multiplayer quiz platform built with my school team. Turn study notes into quizzes with AI, invite players with a game PIN, and compete in live game modes.

TypeScript · Angular · Bun · Socket.IO · PostgreSQL

### [Tripwire](https://github.com/LPuehringerStudent/Hacktoberfest-2026)

A safe place to practise spotting phishing emails and handling scam calls. Gemma powers the scenarios and live callers.

Rust · React · Gemma · ElevenLabs

### [YourSong](https://github.com/MohamadAbdulwahid/YourSong)

An experimental music-discovery app. Pick your favourite few seconds of a song, find similar moments in other tracks, and combine them into a personalised mix.

Python · Flask · Gemini · Audio processing
